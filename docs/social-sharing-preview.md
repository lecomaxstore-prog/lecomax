To prepare the web asset from the generated source, run `node scripts/prepare-social-image.mjs <source-path>`.

Validation: `node --test tests/social-image.test.ts` and `node scripts/verify-share-preview.mjs http://localhost:3012`. The latter requests the rendered homepage as a sharing crawler and checks its metadata and image response. After deployment, run it with `https://lecomax.com`. Messaging services cache previews; old messages may keep their original image even after publication.
