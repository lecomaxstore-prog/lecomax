`CarConcept.glb` is the original, unmodified self-contained binary downloaded from the official sample repository. `provenance.json` records its source URL, SHA-256, exact size and retrieval timestamp. `source-metadata.json` preserves the upstream attribution metadata.

LECOMAX runtime adaptations: semantic part grouping and transform anchors, interactive doors/hood/hatch, camera framing, glass transparency for the embedded alpha showroom, reduced paint normal-map strength, environment lighting and optional user-selected paint colors. Geometry and image textures remain unchanged.

This is a detailed concept car, not a BMW M5, certified CAD or a service manual. Six visual groups are mapped: body, engine, cabin, wheels/axles, brakes and lights. The asset does not provide separate verified transmission, cooling, suspension or differential assemblies. BMW learning notes are presented as comparison material, not as specifications for this model.

Preserve this folder and the in-app source/license attribution when redistributing the model. The license includes its own disclaimer of warranties. The bundler embeds the GLB into the standalone viewer HTML; no Sketchfab SDK, CDN or remote model download is used at runtime.
